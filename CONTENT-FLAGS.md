# Content to confirm with The Khushi Trust

Raised while rebuilding the site from thekhushitrust.org (15 Nov 2026). The new site shows the figures from the programme and impact pages; the old home-page counters disagreed with them.

1. **site.founderName; articles.qari-khushi-urdu.subtitle**: The requested founder field refers to the person in whose memory the Trust was established. The source does not identify the Trust's legal founder. The Urdu article's subtitle calls him a founder of a welfare movement, but the institutional pages describe the Trust as established in his memory. Preserve this distinction.
2. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 158+ Students Supported. This differs from 250 students supported in 2023–24 on the education page and 250+ Total Students on the impact page; no reporting period is supplied for the home number. Removed from the home counters and replaced with a neutral educational-support highlight.
3. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 126+ Women Trained. The women-empowerment page separately reports 150 already trained women working from home and 50+ women trained in 2024–25. The periods and overlap are not explained. Removed from the home counters and replaced with a neutral skills-development highlight; the detailed program figures are retained without adding them together.
4. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 475+ Ration Bags Distributed. The food-assistance and impact pages report 750+ and list 200, 250, and 300 bags for 2023–24, 2024–25, and 2025–26. Removed from the home counters and replaced with neutral food-assistance copy.
5. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 633+ Trees Planted. The tree-plantation and impact pages report 1,000+ trees planted, while the detailed achievement states 1,000 native trees. Removed from the home counters and replaced with neutral tree-plantation copy. Confirm reporting periods and whether the total is exact or a lower bound.
6. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 4+ Healthcare Funds Spent. The number has no currency, scale, or reporting period and conflicts with the PKR 6.8M+ total on the healthcare and impact pages. Removed and replaced with neutral medical-assistance copy.
7. **home.stats**: Source statistic: 633+ Families with Clean Water. The clean-water and impact pages report 1,000+ households served; the detailed achievement says 1,000 household beneficiaries. Removed from the home counters and replaced with neutral water-filtration copy. Confirm the reporting period and household/family terminology.
8. **home.cta.badges**: The source displays '100% Transparent' and 'Direct Impact' without explaining a measurement or an administrative-cost policy. Replaced with the source commitments 'Transparency in fund utilization' and 'Community-driven solutions'; do not interpret the original badge as a zero-overhead claim.
